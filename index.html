The competition

What is STEM Racing?

STEM Racing, formerly known around the world as F1 in Schools, is the world's largest and most prestigious student engineering and enterprise competition.

It is not about building toy cars. Each team runs like a genuine Formula 1 outfit: we research, design in CAD, test in a virtual wind tunnel, manufacture, raise sponsorship, build a brand and present our work to judges.

It is a rigorous, multidisciplinary challenge. Team members take on roles including engineers, project managers, marketing officers and finance directors to run the full team.

How a season works

From a blank sketch to the finish line

  1. Research

    Read the technical regulations, study past cars and decide where time can be found.

  2. Design

    Model the car in Autodesk Fusion, one careful millimetre at a time.

  3. Analyse

    Run airflow simulations to see drag before any material is cut.

  4. Make

    Machine the body on a CNC router, 3D print the wings and wheels, then sand and paint.

  5. Test

    Weigh, measure and run the car. Change one thing, test again.

  6. Race

    Present to the judges, pass scrutineering and line up under the start lights.

More than a race

How teams are judged

The fastest car does not automatically win. Points come from every part of running a team, so every role on our crew matters on competition day.

Categories and weightings change by season and region. We work from the current rules for our competition.

  • ScrutineeringJudges measure the car against the technical regulations. Every dimension out of tolerance costs points.
  • EngineeringHow well we designed, analysed, tested and manufactured the car, and whether we can explain why.
  • Project managementPlanning, teamwork, budgeting and how we handled the problems along the way.
  • EnterpriseTeam identity, pit display, portfolio, marketing and sponsorship.
  • Verbal presentationA timed pitch to the judges covering the whole project.
  • RacingTime trials and reaction races on the 20 m track.

Why a falcon

Built like a stoop

A stoop is the hunting dive of a falcon. The peregrine tucks its wings, turns its body into a teardrop and drops faster than any other animal on Earth, at well over 300 km/h.

That is exactly the problem we are solving: a small body, a short burst of energy, and air that pushes back harder the faster you go. So we put the falcon on our logo and named our mascot Stoop.

Time Attack is the other half of our name. In a time attack there are no rivals on track, only you and the clock.
