    How to win

    Brake in a straight line before the corner, turn in late and get back on the gas early. Stay off the grass: it costs far more time than it looks.

    Your CO₂ tank gives about three and a half seconds of extra thrust and refills slowly. Save it for the exit of a slow corner or the main straight.

    Is this what STEM Racing is like?

    Not quite. A real STEM Racing car has no steering and no driver. It runs dead straight down a 20 metre track on a tether line and is finished in about a second.

    This game imagines our car scaled up to full size and let loose on a circuit. For the real thing, try the time attack simulator.
