What we have learnt so far

Notes from the first class

  • Sketch firstIn Fusion, almost every 3D shape starts as a flat 2D sketch. Get the sketch right and the model follows.
  • Constrain itDimensions and constraints stop a sketch from moving when you change something else. Very useful when the rules give you tolerances.
  • Name thingsA timeline full of "Sketch14" is no help to a teammate. We label our features so anyone can pick up the file.
