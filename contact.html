Quick answers

Asked in the pit lane

What is TA-aero?

Time Attack Aerodynamics is a team of five high school students competing in STEM Racing, the competition formerly known as F1 in Schools.

How fast does the car go?

STEM Racing cars cover the 20 metre track in roughly one second, which means top speeds of around 70 to 80 km/h. Ours is still being designed, so we will post real numbers after our first track test.

How can my company sponsor you?

Get in touch with the team and our Finance Manager will reply with the details. You can also read more on the Sponsors page.

Can you visit our school or event?

We would love to show younger students what the competition is like. Ask us and we will check with our teachers.
