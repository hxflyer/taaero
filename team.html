How we work

One team, three rules

Roles tell us who leads each area. They do not tell us who is allowed to help.

  • Test, don't guessIf two of us disagree about a design, we do not argue. We simulate it or run it.
  • Write it downEvery decision goes in the portfolio. If we cannot explain it to a judge, we have not finished it.
  • Everyone sandsThe boring jobs are shared. So is the credit.
