Why sponsor a school team

Small car, big audience

STEM Racing runs in schools around the world and is supported by Formula 1. When you back a team, your name travels with it.

  • Be seenYour logo on the car, our uniforms, our pit display, our portfolio and this website.
  • Back real skillsYou are funding students learning CAD, manufacturing, budgeting, marketing and public speaking.
  • Get the storyWe send our sponsors updates through the season, with photos you are welcome to share.
